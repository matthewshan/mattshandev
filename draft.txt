For the longest time, I’ve always wanted to build my own portfolio or blog site on Vercel. What’s been my biggest blocker? The motivation to dive into designing the front end (at least in my personal time / outside of a professional setting). Sure there are LOADS of templates out there, but I’ve found molding the template to my needs to be mind numbing. 

This weekend I decided to finally tackle it, and I managed to get it up and running in less than aday. What’s different this time? Context engineering and LLMs are now advance enough to reliability handle these tasks I’ve found boring in the past 

At a high level, this site is powered by NextJS and Vercel. I’ve had experience in NextJS in the past; and Vercel truly makes it mind numbingly easy to get started. 

I made it a little more fun for myself by bootstrapping my Vercel project using Terraform. I also wrote GitHub Workflow for pull request checks that Lints and Builds the application before I can merge int main.
